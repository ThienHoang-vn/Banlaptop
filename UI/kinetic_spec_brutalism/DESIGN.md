## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, technical storefront engineered for hardcore gaming hardware, mobile workstations, and developer computing rigs. Bridging industrial brutalism with tactical cyber-instrumentation, the interface abandons soft consumer conventions in favor of high-density precision, crisp structural framing, and unyielding mechanical clarity.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Engineered for technical operators, competitive gamers, machine learning engineers, and high-output creators who evaluate hardware strictly by thermal efficiency, silicon architecture, memory pipelines, and benchmark metrics. The UI evokes the visceral authority of a calibrated flight deck or military-grade diagnostic suite: uncompromising, reliable, and laser-focused on throughput.

### Movement & Aesthetic Direction
- **Industrial Brutalism:** Exact, unyielding, razor-sharp 90-degree corners. Zero border radius throughout the interface. Heavy, mechanical segment dividers simulate modular rack-mount hardware panels.
- **Instrument-Grade Information Density:** Technical telemetry layouts where product cards prioritize chip architecture, thermal wattage (TDP), bus speeds, and configuration matrices rather than ambiguous marketing fluff.
- **Cyber-Tactile Interaction:** Micro-interactions mimic optical terminal switches. States snap instantly with zero-latency visual displacement, rigid crosshairs, high-contrast borders, and high-frequency active feedback states.

## Colors

The palette operates under a high-contrast industrial light theme, blending clinical field environments with electric computational telemetry.

### Core Swatches & Role Allocation
- **Primary (`#2563EB` - Electric Royal Blue):** Primary actions, tactical focus frames, active bus selectors, and verified spec highlights. Radiates hyper-responsive computing power against sterile surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` - Deep Void Navy):** Structural headers, monospaced tech framing, chassis indicators, high-contrast typography, and uncompromising anchor zones.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Tactical Terminal Green):** Instant conversion telemetry, live physical inventory trackers ('IN STOCK - WAREHOUSE DEPLOYMENT'), active system power states, and high-urgency consultation triggers (Direct Tech Desk/WhatsApp/Zalo).
- **Neutral Accent (`#0284C7` - Cyberspace Blue):** Secondary filters, spec micro-chips, thermal readouts, active toggle state borders, and secondary telemetry accents.
- **Background Architecture (`#F8FAFC` & `#FFFFFF`):** High-reflectivity clinical gray baseline (`#F8FAFC`) framed by dense structural white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) with distinct mechanical separation.

### Functional States
- **Hover/Interaction:** Snaps instantly to high-voltage inverted fills or aggressive `#1D4ED8` borders. No soft gradient decays.
- **Disabled/Locked Out:** `#E2E8F0` ground with `#94A3B8` mechanical diagonal striping or muted monospaced text indicators (`[OFFLINE]`).

## Typography

Typography functions as visual instrumentation. The pairing delivers severe technical impact and ergonomic readability across dense specification matrices.

### Font Pair Strategy
- **Headlines & Interface Telemetry (Space Grotesk):** Provides a sharp geometric, computational cadence. Proportional tabular behaviors, mechanical alternates, and rigid letter forms communicate precision engineering. All primary metrics, hardware tags, SKU markers, and prices are set in Space Grotesk.
- **Analytical Body Text (Hanken Grotesk):** Provides clear optical balance and horizontal tracking for sustained reading during configuration analysis, benchmark reviews, and procurement audits.

### Numerical & Specification Styling
- Spec matrix items (e.g., `RTX 4090 175W TDP`, `64GB DDR5 5600MHz`, `PCIe Gen4 2TB`) use `spec-code` styling: all-caps, medium/bold weight, with tabular number alignment to ensure benchmark comparison columns never drift.
- Strike-through comparison prices feature a heavy structural line through the numeric centerline with 60% opacity against the secondary tone.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a 12-column modular framework constructed with brutalist gridlines and high-density information units.

### Modular Grid & Framing
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid. Filter facets lock into a fixed 3-column tactical sidebar (25%), feeding a high-density 9-column catalog quadrant (75%) displaying 3-across workstation/laptop matrices.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Facets collapse into an off-canvas slide-out mechanical panel with top-line active telemetry chips; product grid re-flows to 2-across.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 0.75rem gutters. Product cards stretch to single-column blocks with full-bleed technical specs arranged in 2x2 internal data tiles.

### Spacing Discipline
Spacing conforms strictly to a 4px/8px industrial cadence. Padding within interactive components is compact and rigid, maximizing vertical efficiency to eliminate gratuitous scrolling when comparing hardware SKUs.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects soft, diffused drop shadows and blurry ambient glows. Depth is expressed purely through structural layering, hard solid offset silhouettes, and crisp architectural borders.

### Mechanical Elevation Planes
- **Ground Floor (Base):** Flat `#F8FAFC` foundation cross-hatched by subtle `#E2E8F0` structural guide boundaries.
- **Surface Elevation (Cards & Panels):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed in a mandatory 1px or 2px solid border of `#0F172A` or `#CBD5E1`.
- **Active / Hover State Displacement (Hard Drop):** When an element rises in hierarchy, it receives an intentional hard-edge drop offset: a 2px horizontal, 2px vertical solid offset block (`box-shadow: 3px 3px 0px #0F172A`), physically simulating mechanical switches or tactile punch-card interfaces.
- **Floating Modals & Lead Overlays:** Solid 2px `#0F172A` perimeter with an assertive 6px 6px 0px `#0F172A` drop shadow, completely sharp and un-blurred.
- **Focus Rings:** High-visibility double outline: 2px pure white inner gap with a 2px `#2563EB` solid exterior line.

## Shapes

The geometry of this design system is absolute and razor-sharp. Roundedness is locked at `0`.

### Geometric Principles
- **True Zero Radii:** Every button, card container, badge, drop-down menu, filter pill, input field, and modal overlay has a border-radius of precisely `0px`.
- **Chiseled Construction:** Where dynamic visual hierarchy is required, elements may utilize 45-degree corner cutouts (dog-ear chamfers) achieved via CSS clip-path or angled SVG overlays, reinforcing military-grade chassis and industrial heatsink motifs.
- **Dividers & Structural Rulers:** All separators are sharp 1px or 2px rules. Sub-panel headers feature micro notch tabs or mechanical tick-marks along the outer borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("DEPLOY HARDWARE" / "BUY NOW"):** Solid `#2563EB` background, `#FFFFFF` text, Space Grotesk bold, all-caps, 0px radius. Hover triggers an instant snap: translates `-2px, -2px` with a solid `2px 2px 0px #0F172A` offset shadow. Active state depresses to `0px, 0px`.
- **Secondary Lead Conversion ("CONNECT TECH DESK / WHATSAPP / ZALO"):** Solid `#10B981` background or crisp `#0F172A` border with `#10B981` text, displaying a pulsing 6px x 6px green square indicator next to live representative status.
- **Tertiary / Action Framing:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#0F172A`, text `#0F172A`. Inverts to solid `#0F172A` with white text on hover.

### Hardware Spec Badges & Chips
- **Structural Spec Chips (CPU, RAM, GPU, SSD):** Compact rectangular tiles (`roundedness: 0`). Background `#F1F5F9`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, Space Grotesk bold 11px. Layout format: `[CHIP ICON / LABEL] [VALUE]`, e.g., `GPU // RTX 4080 12GB`.
- **Stock Status Badge:** 0px radius pill substitute. Border 1px solid `#10B981`, background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, font `spec-code`. Prepended with an active ASCII or solid square beacon: `■ IN STOCK - READY FOR COURIER`.

### Product & Matrix Cards
- **Construction:** Pure `#FFFFFF` card body surrounded by 1px solid `#E2E8F0`. 
- **Header:** Category and SKU code displayed in uppercase `spec-code` `#64748B`.
- **Media Quadrant:** Clean, isolated product render positioned on a sterile light gray grid backplate.
- **Spec Matrix Rack:** A 2x2 or 4x1 segmented technical table separating CPU, GPU, Display Hz/Resolution, and Memory. Every item boxed with a micro 1px outline.
- **Price Block:** Strike-through MSRP in `#64748B` body font; current cash/procurement price in `#0F172A` Space Grotesk 24px bold.
- **Hover Interaction:** Card border transitions from `#E2E8F0` to `#2563EB` with an immediate `4px 4px 0px #0F172A` solid brutalist shadow.

### Input Fields & Lead Forms
- **Field Base:** `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px solid `#0F172A`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`, 0px radius. Placeholder text `#94A3B8`. Space Grotesk label anchored directly above in `label-caps` styling.
- **Active Focus:** Instant border expansion to 2px solid `#2563EB` with zero animation delay.
- **Direct Conversion Drawer / Consultation Forms:** Clean modal overlay with `#0F172A` high-contrast header block, tabbed configuration summary, and rapid one-click communication triggers (WhatsApp, Zalo, Direct Callback).

### Checkboxes & Segmented Filter Toggles
- **Checkboxes:** Sharp 16px x 16px square, 1px solid `#0F172A`. Checked state fills `#2563EB` with an inverted white checkmark or inner solid square.
- **Filter Switchers:** Monospaced segment blocks welded together. The active selection inverts to solid `#0F172A` with `#FFFFFF` text.
